# Voice

**Status: not in Nimbus-One, by design.**

No speech-to-text, no text-to-speech, no voice notes, no mic button.
The product is five text tools through one loop; audio engines
(whisper models, TTS binaries, browser capture) are exactly the kind
of heavy, platform-dependent surface the scope lock excludes.

If voice is ever approved, it arrives as its own tested feature with
its own security review — not as an extension to `run`. Until then,
dictate into the `run` prompt with your OS tools.
